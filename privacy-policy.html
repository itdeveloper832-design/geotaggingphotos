Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Welcome to GeoTaggingPhotos. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring transparency about how our platform operates. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect information when you use our website and photo geotagging tools.

By accessing or using GeoTaggingPhotos, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

info 1. About GeoTaggingPhotos

GeoTaggingPhotos provides online tools that allow users to add, edit, inspect, and manage GPS coordinates and EXIF metadata within digital images. Our services include:

• Photo geotagging • Bulk image geotagging • EXIF metadata viewing • GPS coordinate editing • Image location discovery • Metadata management tools

Our goal is to provide secure, fast, and privacy-focused tools for photographers, businesses, travelers, drone operators, researchers, and content creators around the world.

lock 2. Client-Side Image Processing

Privacy is one of our core principles. GeoTaggingPhotos is designed to process images directly inside your browser whenever possible.

check_circle Your images remain under your control.
check_circle GPS coordinates and EXIF metadata are processed locally.
check_circle We do not permanently store your uploaded images.

check_circle We do not access your personal photo library.

Image processing may use browser technologies such as JavaScript, WebAssembly, and local device resources to perform geotagging and metadata editing operations.

my_location 4. Location Data and EXIF Metadata

GeoTaggingPhotos provides tools for editing GPS coordinates and photo metadata. If you choose to use features such as Use Current Location, your browser may ask for permission to access your device's location.

check Your location is accessed only after your explicit consent.
check You can deny location access at any time.
check We do not permanently store your GPS coordinates.
check We do not track your movements.
check You control whether location data is added to your images.

cookie5. Cookies, Storage, and Analytics

The public site does not require a login or a server-side photo account. The PWA may use browser localStorage to remember installation prompts and interface state. The service worker caches application resources for offline use; it is not intended to cache user-selected photos.

The site may load third-party fonts and libraries from CDNs. Map and place-search features use OpenStreetMap tiles or geocoding services when you request them. These requests can include map coordinates or search text, but the image file itself is processed locally by the browser.

extension 6. Third-Party Services

Our website may link to or load third-party resources such as fonts, JavaScript libraries, map tiles, and geocoding services. Their own policies govern those requests. The current static tool does not upload photos to a photo-processing backend.

GeoTaggingPhotos does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to advertisers or third parties.
