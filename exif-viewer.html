What is EXIF Metadata and What Information is Stored in Photos?

EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) is a global standard for digital photography metadata used by digital cameras, smartphones, drones, and imaging software. Every time a photograph is taken, embedded technical headers are recorded directly inside the file containing vital technical parameters about the camera hardware, exposure settings, and geographic coordinates:

photo_camera

Camera Hardware Details

Extracts camera manufacturer make, exact model name, lens specifications, focal length, color space, orientation, and resolution details.

tune

Exposure Settings

Inspects shutter speed, aperture (f-number), ISO sensitivity, exposure compensation, metering mode, flash status, and white balance parameters.

pin_drop

GPS Location Tags

Decodes embedded GPS latitude, longitude, altitude, GPS timestamps, and satellite positioning precision headers recorded during capture.

Why Inspect Photo EXIF Metadata?

Inspecting metadata provides essential insights for photographers, digital investigators, privacy advocates, and e-commerce professionals alike:

shield Privacy & Location Security Audits

Check whether your personal photos contain precise residential GPS coordinates or sensitive capture timestamps before sharing them online or sending them to third parties.

school Photography Skill & Lighting Analysis

Analyze the exact shutter speed, ISO, and lens aperture settings of inspiring photographs to understand lighting techniques and master manual camera control.

verified_user Forensic & Copyright Verification

Verify camera serial numbers, original creation dates, and editing software signatures to prove photo copyright ownership or identify image tampering.

dataset GIS & Drone Asset Cataloging

Audit raw spatial coordinates embedded by UAV drones and mobile cameras for seamless import into ArcGIS, QGIS, or digital asset management software.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about viewing photo metadata and inspecting embedded camera EXIF tags.

How can I check if my photo contains hidden GPS coordinates?

Upload your photo into our Online EXIF Viewer. The tool reads the file binary instantly and displays any embedded GPS latitude, longitude, altitude, and an interactive satellite map marker if location tags are present.

What camera settings are revealed by the EXIF viewer?

The viewer extracts camera manufacturer, camera model, lens type, focal length, aperture (f-stop), shutter speed, ISO sensitivity, exposure bias, and original capture date and time.

Are my photos uploaded to a server when checking EXIF data?

No. All EXIF parsing is executed 100% client-side inside your browser memory using JavaScript. Your images never leave your local device.

Why is some EXIF metadata missing from my photo?

Metadata may be missing if location services were disabled during shooting, or if the photo was downloaded from social media platforms (like WhatsApp, Instagram, or Facebook) that automatically strip EXIF headers for privacy.
