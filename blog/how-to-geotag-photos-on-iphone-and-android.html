How to Geotag Photos on iPhone and Android: Complete Mobile Guide

Step-by-step instructions to enable automatic GPS tagging, add location to existing photos, and edit EXIF metadata on iOS and Android smartphones without installing bloated apps.

calendar_today Updated August 2026 • schedule 4 min read
lightbulb Quick Answer: How to Geotag Photos on Mobile

To automatically geotag future photos on iPhone, enable Location Services > Camera in iOS Settings. On Android, open Camera Settings and toggle on Location Tags / Geotagging. To add or fix coordinates on existing photos, use the free browser tool at GeoTaggingPhotos.com on your phone to inject exact decimal GPS coordinates directly into the photo EXIF header in 1 click.

smartphone How Photo Geotagging Works on Smartphones

Every time you take a photo with your smartphone, the device has the capability to capture GPS telemetry (latitude, longitude, altitude, and heading) and store it in the EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) metadata block embedded inside the image file.

However, location tagging is often disabled by default to protect battery life or user privacy, or coordinates can be stripped when photos are edited, converted from HEIC to JPG, or downloaded from cloud backups and social platforms like WhatsApp and Instagram.

phone_iphone Part 1: How to Geotag Photos on iPhone (iOS 16 and later)

1 Enable Automatic Camera Location Tagging

To ensure all future photos carry GPS data:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Scroll down and tap Privacy & Security > Location Services.
  3. Ensure Location Services is switched On.
  4. Scroll down to Camera, select While Using the App, and ensure Precise Location is toggled On.

2 Add or Adjust Location on Existing iPhone Photos

If you have photos in your Apple Photos library that were taken without GPS coordinates:

  1. Open the Photos app and select the image.
  2. Swipe up on the photo or tap the (i) Info button at the bottom.
  3. Tap Add a Location... (or Adjust if a location already exists).
  4. Type the address, landmark, or city name and select the correct result.

android Part 2: How to Geotag Photos on Android (Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi)

1 Turn On Camera Location Tags in Android Camera

  1. Open the native Camera app on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the Settings gear icon (usually top-left or top-right).
  3. Look for Location tags, Save location, or Geotagging and toggle it On.
  4. When prompted by Android, grant the camera app permission to access device location.

2 Add or Edit Location in Google Photos

  1. Open Google Photos and tap the photo you wish to geotag.
  2. Swipe up or tap the three dots (⋮) menu.
  3. Tap Add a location (or tap the edit pencil next to the map).
  4. Search for the location and confirm.

How to Turn Location On for Photos (iOS and Android)

On iPhone, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Location Services, and switch Location Services on. Then find Camera in the same list and set it to While Using the App, so photos you take from now on carry GPS coordinates.

On Android, open Settings, then Location, and switch Use Location on. Then open the camera app's permission settings and allow Location while using the app. Exact menu names vary slightly by phone maker. Note that turning location on only affects photos taken afterwards; older photos do not gain GPS data retroactively.

Why are my iPhone photos not showing a location? expand_more

Location Services may be disabled for the Camera app. Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Camera and choose While Using the App with Precise Location enabled.

Can I remove GPS location before sharing photos on social media? expand_more

Yes. Most platforms strip metadata upon upload, but to protect your privacy before sending original files, you can use our Add Geotag Tool and choose the "Remove GPS" option.
