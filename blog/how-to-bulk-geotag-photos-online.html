How to Bulk Geotag Photos Online: Fast Batch GPS Guide

Save hours of manual editing by adding GPS coordinates to multiple photos simultaneously. Learn how to batch process up to 500 images directly in your browser with instant ZIP export.

calendar_today Updated August 2026 • schedule 4 min read
bolt Quick Summary: How to Bulk Geotag Photos Online

To bulk geotag multiple photos online without installing software: open the GeoTaggingPhotos Bulk Geotagging Tool, drag and drop up to 500 images, enter your target latitude and longitude once (or click "Use Current Location"), click Process Batch, and download all updated files instantly bundled into a clean ZIP archive.

view_timeline Step-by-Step: How to Batch Geotag Up to 500 Photos

1 Upload Your Photo Batch

Open the Bulk Geotagging Suite. Drag and drop all images from your folder directly onto the upload zone. You can mix JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and TIFF files.

2 Enter Coordinates or Pick on Map

Input the latitude (e.g. 40.7128) and longitude (e.g. -74.0060), or click Use Current Location. The coordinates will automatically be assigned to every image in your batch list.

3 Process & Download ZIP

Click Process Batch. The browser modifies the EXIF headers client-side in seconds and automatically generates a downloadable geotagged_photos.zip bundle.

Troubleshooting: When a File Won't Join the Queue

If a photo does not appear in the queue after you select it, check the format first. The tool accepts JPG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, and GIF files, and anything else is filtered out. Batches are capped at 500 files per session, so files beyond that limit are dropped. Very large batches can also take a moment to register, so give the page a few seconds before you re-select. If the queue still looks empty, re-select the files; the Process button itself re-reads the selected files before it starts, so a missed update does not block you. What Happens to Image Quality in Bulk Mode

Bulk mode works differently from single-photo EXIF editing. Each image is redrawn and saved as a JPEG at quality 0.95, and then the GPS coordinates are written into the new file. The quality change is visually negligible at that setting, but it is technically a re-encode, not a metadata-only edit. That is why the tool never touches your originals: the files on your device stay exactly as they were, and only the downloaded ZIP contains the processed copies. If pixel-perfect originals matter to you, keep them, and treat the ZIP as the sharing set.

Processed files are renamed with a _geotagged suffix and bundled into a single ZIP archive called geotagged-photos.zip, so a 500-file batch comes back as one download instead of 500 separate files.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many photos can I bulk geotag at the same time?

GeoTaggingPhotos supports batch processing up to 500 images per session directly in your browser.

Are my images uploaded to external servers during bulk geotagging?

No. All batch EXIF metadata modification happens locally on your computer/device using JavaScript Web APIs, ensuring 100% privacy.

Does bulk geotagging compress or resize my images?

Bulk mode redraws each image and saves it as a high-quality JPEG (quality 0.95) before writing the GPS data, so there is a minimal, visually negligible re-compression. Your original files on your device are never overwritten.
