view_timeline Step-by-Step: How to Batch Geotag Up to 500 Photos

1 Upload Your Photo Batch

Open the Bulk Geotagging Suite. Drag and drop all images from your folder directly onto the upload zone. You can mix JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and TIFF files.

2 Enter Coordinates or Pick on Map

Input the latitude (e.g. 40.7128) and longitude (e.g. -74.0060), or click Use Current Location. The coordinates will automatically be assigned to every image in your batch list.

3 Process & Download ZIP

Click Process Batch. The browser modifies the EXIF headers client-side in seconds and automatically generates a downloadable geotagged_photos.zip bundle.
