How to Add GPS Coordinates to Photos Online

Photo geotagging is the process of embedding geographic coordinates—specifically latitude, longitude, and altitude—directly into digital photos and image files. Whether you captured photographs with a DSLR or mirrorless camera that lacks built-in GPS hardware, or you need to add location metadata to images for mapping, real estate, and project documentation, our free online photo geotagging tool makes the process instant and effortless:

1

Upload Your Image

Drag and drop your photo into the geotagging editor above. The tool supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and TIFF images up to 50MB per file. Every file is processed 100% locally in your browser memory.

2

Select or Adjust GPS Location

Click anywhere on the interactive map to place a geotag pin, drag the blue marker to fine-tune your position, enter exact latitude and longitude values manually, or click "Use Current Location" for instant device detection.

3

Save & Download Geotagged Photo

Click "Add Coordinates to Photo" and then "Download Photo". Your updated image is generated instantly with the new EXIF GPS tags embedded without quality degradation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about adding GPS coordinates and location metadata to photos.

How do I add GPS coordinates to a photo online?

Upload your photo to GeoTaggingPhotos.com/add-geotag, click or drag the blue pin on the interactive map (or click 'Use Current Location'), and click 'Add Coordinates to Photo' to download your updated image with embedded EXIF GPS tags.

Does adding GPS coordinates reduce photo quality?

No. The tool directly updates the EXIF metadata segments in your photo's binary structure without recompressing or degrading the original image pixels.

Can I replace existing GPS coordinates on a photo?

Yes. When you upload a photo with existing GPS data, the editor automatically reads the coordinates and allows you to overwrite them with a new map position or remove them entirely.

Is it safe to geotag sensitive photos online?

Yes. All metadata editing and EXIF processing occurs 100% locally in your browser session. No photos or location coordinates are uploaded to external servers.
