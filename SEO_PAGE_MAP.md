## Canonical ownership rules

The homepage owns the broad category. `/add-geotag` owns the action of adding, changing, or removing GPS on an existing file. `/exif-viewer` owns inspection and “check GPS” tool intent, while the new article owns the question/how-to intent. `/image-location-finder` owns mapping embedded coordinates and explicitly does not claim visual AI geolocation. `/bulk-geotagging` owns batch processing and ZIP output. The two existing how-to guides and the new GPS-check guide each have distinct problems and link to the matching tool.

No URL was created for every synonym, device, city, or country. The architecture uses a small number of pages with clear intent ownership, as recommended by the supplied requirements.
