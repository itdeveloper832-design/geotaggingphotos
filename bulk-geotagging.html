How to Geotag Multiple Photos in Bulk Online

Manually injecting GPS coordinates into dozens of individual photos can take hours. Our browser-based Bulk Geotagging Tool lets you apply latitude, longitude, altitude, and custom EXIF metadata across hundreds of images simultaneously in three simple steps:

1

Batch Upload Photos

Select or drag up to 500 images into the drop zone. Supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP file formats up to 50MB each (TIFF and HEIC in Safari).

2

Set Global Coordinates

Enter your target latitude and longitude values or click "Current Location". The coordinates are automatically applied across every queued file.

3

Export as ZIP Archive

Click "Process & Download ZIP". The tool embeds binary EXIF GPS data into every image and packages your files into a single ZIP download.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about batch geotagging photos and managing large image collections.

How many photos can I geotag in a single bulk batch?

You can upload and batch-process up to 500 photos simultaneously per session directly in your browser without file size paywalls.

How do I download the batch of geotagged images?

Once you enter your coordinate preset and click 'Process & Download ZIP', all updated images are automatically compiled into a single organized ZIP archive and downloaded to your device.

Are my batch photo uploads sent to a cloud server?

No. All batch file reading, canvas rendering, EXIF GPS injection, and ZIP packaging occur 100% locally inside your web browser. No image files are transmitted to external servers.

What image formats are supported for bulk geotagging?

The bulk geotagger supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP, and TIFF image formats, applying consistent GPS coordinates and optional metadata across all files.
