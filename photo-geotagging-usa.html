Overview

Professional Photo Geotagging Across the United States

From New York and Los Angeles to Chicago and Miami, millions of photos are captured every day using smartphones, cameras, drones, and professional equipment. These images often contain valuable metadata, including GPS coordinates, altitude, timestamps, and camera information.

GeoTaggingPhotos helps photographers, businesses, real-estate professionals, and content creators add, edit, replace, and manage photo location data in seconds. Whether you're photographing properties in Texas, documenting projects in California, or capturing landscapes in Colorado, accurate GPS metadata helps organize and manage your images more efficiently.

Our platform supports both single-image editing and bulk photo processing, allowing users to update hundreds of photos simultaneously without complicated software or technical knowledge.

With support for JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP (TIFF and HEIC work in Safari only) formats, GeoTaggingPhotos works perfectly for travel photography, aerial surveys, property listings, and commercial projects across the United States.
