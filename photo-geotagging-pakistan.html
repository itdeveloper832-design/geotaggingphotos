Overview

Professional Photo Geotagging Across Pakistan

From Karachi and Lahore to Islamabad and Peshawar, millions of photos are captured daily across Pakistan using smartphones, DSLR cameras, and aerial drones. These photos hold crucial EXIF metadata including GPS latitude, longitude, altitude, and timestamp information.

GeoTaggingPhotos enables Pakistani photographers, real estate agencies, construction firms, and travel creators to manage geotags with speed and precision. Whether you are photographing housing projects in DHA or Bahria Town, documenting northern mountain expeditions in Gilgit-Baltistan, or logging civil infrastructure, accurate spatial tags keep your content organized.

Supports lossless client-side batch editing and instantaneous ZIP exports.
