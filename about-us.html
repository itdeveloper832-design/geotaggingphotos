Empowering Photo Metadata Management for Creators Around the World

GeoTaggingPhotos.com was created to make photo geotagging, GPS metadata editing, and EXIF management simple, fast, and accessible for everyone. From photographers and travelers to real-estate agencies, drone operators, and mapping professionals, our mission is to provide powerful tools that help users add, inspect, replace, and organize location data directly from their browsers.

Our platform enables users to upload single or multiple images, edit latitude and longitude coordinates, inspect hidden EXIF metadata, discover photo locations, and process large image collections without installing complicated software. Whether you're documenting travel memories, managing property listings, or organizing aerial photography, GeoTaggingPhotos.com streamlines the entire workflow.

Built with performance, privacy, and usability in mind, our tools support common browser-readable image formats. Exact output behavior can vary by format, so the application explains when a file is exported as JPEG.

Why We Built GeoTaggingPhotos.com

Photo location data can be useful for organizing albums, documenting sites, and reviewing image files, but it is often hidden inside EXIF metadata. We created GeoTaggingPhotos.com to give photographers, businesses, and creators complete control over their photo location data without requiring expensive desktop software or advanced technical knowledge.

Our goal is simple: provide an easy-to-use platform where users can:

add_location_alt Add GPS coordinates to photos.
edit_location Replace existing latitude and longitude values.
manage_search View and inspect EXIF metadata.
travel_explore Find the location where an image was captured.
photo_library Process hundreds of images simultaneously.
download_for_offline Download edited photos instantly.

Whether you need to organize travel albums, manage real-estate listings, or verify the origin of an image, our platform is designed to make metadata management effortless.

Our Core Principles

lock

🔒 Privacy-First Processing

Your privacy is our highest priority. Images and metadata are processed directly inside your browser whenever possible, giving you complete control over your files. The public tool is designed to process photo files in the browser rather than upload them to a photo-processing backend. Map and geocoding requests are separate network requests made only when those features are used.
