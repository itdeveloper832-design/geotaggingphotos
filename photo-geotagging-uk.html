Overview

Professional Photo Geotagging Across the United Kingdom

From London and Manchester to Edinburgh and Belfast, millions of photos are captured every day using smartphones, digital cameras, and drones. These images often contain valuable metadata, including GPS coordinates, timestamps, altitude, and camera settings.

GeoTaggingPhotos helps photographers, businesses, and property professionals add, edit, and manage location information with ease. Whether you're documenting a construction project in Birmingham, photographing properties in London, or capturing landscapes in Scotland, accurate GPS metadata helps keep your images organized and searchable.

Our platform allows users to upload one image or process hundreds of files simultaneously. Instead of manually editing each photo, you can apply the same coordinates to an entire collection in seconds.

The editor supports popular image formats, including JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP (TIFF and HEIC work in Safari only), making it suitable for travel photography, real-estate marketing, aerial photography, and commercial projects throughout the United Kingdom.
