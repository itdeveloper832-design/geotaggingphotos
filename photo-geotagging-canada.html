Overview

Professional Photo Geotagging Across Canada

From Toronto and Vancouver to Montreal and Calgary, millions of photos are captured every day across Canada using smartphones, digital cameras, and drones. These images contain vital metadata, including GPS latitude and longitude, altitude, timestamps, and camera parameters.

GeoTaggingPhotos helps Canadian photographers, real estate boards, environmental researchers, and businesses add, edit, and manage location tags efficiently. Whether you're documenting commercial properties in Ontario, filming aerial drone footage in British Columbia, or logging site progress in Alberta, accurate spatial metadata keeps your media organized and easily searchable.

Our platform supports single-file location updating as well as bulk photo processing, enabling users to apply verified coordinates to hundreds of images in seconds.

Supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WebP (TIFF and HEIC work in Safari only) image formats without requiring software downloads.
